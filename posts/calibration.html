Why Neural Networks Are Overconfident

A mathematical introduction to predictive confidence and calibration.

Draft template: This is an example article scaffold, not a completed or published research article. Replace it with your original writing before sharing publicly.

1. The question

A classifier may report 90% confidence without being correct on 90% of such predictions. Why? What does confidence mean, and how should it be evaluated?

2. Softmax probabilities

For logits \(z_k(x)\), a multiclass classifier predicts:

\[p_\theta(y=k\mid x)=\frac{e^{z_k(x)}}{\sum_j e^{z_j(x)}}.\]

Normalization does not by itself guarantee calibrated probabilities.

3. Confidence calibration

Let \(\hat Y\) denote the predicted class and \(C\) its associated probability. Confidence calibration requires:

\[\mathbb{P}(Y=\hat Y\mid C=c)=c.\]
